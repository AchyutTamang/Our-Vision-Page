The Artistry of Anisha Maharjan

Anisha Maharjan is a singer/songwriter, composer and music educator. Her objective is teaching and learning music, experimenting, discovering and transferring music. During her career, she has collaborated with national and international artists.

Her interest in music started at a very young age. She has been trained under Marcus Dengate, David Pudney, Devin Rahman (Australia), Charlotte Jacobs (Belgium) and Maria Fajardo (Spain) and eastern classical music under Dhan Bahadur Gopali (Kalanidhi Indira Sangeet Mahavidhyalaya) and Bishal Bhattarai (Kathmandu Dhrupad gurukul).

In 2013, Anisha won the "Songs Nepal, Acoustic Festival 2013" and has participated in International Jazz Day since 2015. In 2019, she was awarded the UNESCO Scholarship for Women, the Kathmandu Jazz Conservatory Scholarship, and the Jamey Aebersold Scholarship for the Thailand Jazz Workshop.

Her points of interest include jazz, ballad, soul, experimental music, and eastern classical vocals.

Anisha finished her jazz vocals diploma from Kathmandu Jazz Conservatory in 2019 and is studying for a Master of Music at Excelsia College in Sydney, Australia. She has worked as a vocal and music theory teacher and choir leader for pop diploma students at the Conservatory and currently teaches at Maxx Music in Australia.

Music Education

Teaching Philosophy

Anisha believes in a holistic approach to music education, combining technical training with creative expression. Her teaching methodology encourages students to discover their unique voice while building a strong foundation in music theory and vocal techniques.

  • Personalized vocal coaching for all skill levels
  • Music theory and composition instruction
  • Eastern and Western vocal techniques
  • Choir direction and ensemble training
